Zenz

A free app for playing classic text adventures

Zenz plays interactive fiction: the text adventures of the 1980s, such as Zork and The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy, and the thousands of free games written by the community since. You read the story, type what you want to do, and the game answers.

Zenz is made by one person, Trevor Leybourne, in his own time. It is free to use.

What it does

  • A map that draws itself as you explore, with the ways you have found between rooms.
  • Quick picks of the exits, verbs and things around you, so you can play by tapping as well as typing.
  • A library of your games with their saves, and saving where you left off, every turn.
  • Finding free games from the Interactive Fiction Database and the IF Archive, where their authors have shared them.
  • Themes to choose the colours you read in.

About the games

Zenz is a player, like a music player: it doesn't come with games and doesn't sell them. It only downloads games that their authors have made free, from the public IF Archive, and refuses anything commercial. For older commercial games, such as Infocom's, you use copies you already own.
